What is in the book

The planned contents, which may change before publication:

  1. Hello, SaiLang. Installing, running your first program, and saying things.
  2. Words that hold values. Variables, numbers, and text.
  3. Making decisions. when and otherwise.
  4. Doing things again. Loops, stop and skip.
  5. Lists. Keeping many things together.
  6. Commands of your own. Inputs, results, and recursion.
  7. When things go wrong. Errors, try, and reading messages.
  8. Files and modules. Saving notes and reusing code.
  9. Windows and pictures. Building a small desktop app.
  10. Your first game. A coin-collecting game from start to finish.

Who it is for

Children aged about 9 and over who can read well, adults who have never programmed, and teachers who want a gentle first language. No previous programming experience is needed.
